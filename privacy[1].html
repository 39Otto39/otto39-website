Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Otto39 is an independent music project. Its private YouTube publishing application is used by the channel owner to upload original music videos to the Otto39 YouTube channel.

Google data access

When the channel owner authorizes the application with Google, the application requests the YouTube upload permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload) to upload videos to the authorized YouTube channel. The application is not intended for public users.

Data use and storage

Google authorization credentials may be stored locally on the owner's computer to maintain authorized access. They are not published on this website or sold to third parties. The application uses Google account authorization only to upload videos as directed by the channel owner.

Data sharing

Otto39 does not sell Google user data. Data is shared with Google/YouTube only as necessary to perform the authorized video upload operation.

Revoking access

The channel owner may revoke the application's access at Google Account permissions. To request information or deletion of locally stored credentials, contact the project owner.
